@@Navigation Conversions
The followind non-standard conversions are supported for navigation calculations.
####
ç{AccentText}ƒ{1.0}Degrees of Longitude <-> Timeƒ{}ç{}

Longitude in degrees, DMS, DM or minutes can be converted to hours or Hours:Min:Sec.
One hour corresponds to 15 degrees, so 24h makes 360 degrees.
####
ç{AccentText}ƒ{1.0}Degrees of Latitude <-> Nautical Milesƒ{}ç{}

Degrees, DMS, DM or minutes of angle convert to nautical miles.
One minute of latitude equals one nautical mile.
